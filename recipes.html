Charlie's Berry cobbler

In Twilight, Charlie Swan often cooks simple, comforting meals for Bella, and berry cobbler fits his practical, homey style.The pie is simple and rustic, with a golden, slightly crisp topping that cracks as it bakes. Beneath it, warm berries soften and burst, releasing a deep, sweet-tart juice that smells of summer and comfort.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups mixed berries (fresh or frozen)
  • ½ cup sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1½ tsp baking powder
  • ¼ tsp salt
  • ½ cup milk
  • ½ cup melted butter

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Mix berries with half the sugar and lemon juice. Spread in a baking dish.
  3. Make batter: In a bowl, mix flour, baking powder, salt, remaining sugar, milk, and melted butter.
  4. Pour batter evenly over the berries (don’t stir).
  5. Bake for 35–40 minutes until golden and bubbly.
  6. Cool slightly and serve warm (great with ice cream).

Mushroom Ravioli

Tender pasta pockets filled with earthy mushrooms, served in a rich, flavorful sauce; indulgent and elegant, the dish highlights a rare, intimate, and “normal” dining moment between Bella and Edward.

Ingredients

  • Ingredients – Filling
  • 250 g mushrooms (button, cremini, or mixed), finely chopped
  • 1 small onion or shallot, finely chopped
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil or butter
  • ½ cup ricotta cheese
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Ingredients – Ravioli
  • Fresh pasta sheets (store-bought or homemade)
  • 1 egg (for sealing)
  • To serve (optional)
  • Butter or olive oil
  • Extra Parmesan
  • Fresh herbs (parsley or thyme)

Steps

  1. Cook mushrooms: Heat oil or butter in a pan, sauté onion until soft. Add garlic and mushrooms, cook until moisture evaporates. Season with salt and pepper. Let cool.
  2. Make filling: Mix cooked mushrooms with ricotta and Parmesan. Adjust seasoning.
  3. Prepare ravioli: Lay out pasta sheets and place small spoonfuls of filling evenly.
  4. Seal: Brush edges with beaten egg, cover with another pasta sheet, press around filling, and cut ravioli.
  5. Cook: Boil in salted water for 2–3 minutes until ravioli float.
  6. Serve: Toss with butter or olive oil and top with Parmesan and herbs.

Chicken Enchiladas

In the Twilight books, heroine Bella Swan contemplates the mysterious Edward Cullen as she prepares chicken enchiladas for dinner. Soft tortillas filled with seasoned chicken, smothered in savory enchilada sauce and melted cheese; warm, hearty, and comforting, perfect for a satisfying meal.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked chicken, shredded
  • 1 cup enchilada sauce (red or green)
  • 1 cup shredded cheese (cheddar, Monterey Jack, or mix)
  • 8 small tortillas (corn or flour)
  • ½ small onion, finely chopped (optional)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Prepare filling: Heat oil in a pan, sauté onion until soft. Add chicken, salt, pepper, and 2–3 tbsp enchilada sauce. Mix well.
  3. Fill tortillas: Spoon chicken mixture into each tortilla, sprinkle with cheese, and roll up.
  4. Assemble: Place enchiladas seam-side down in a baking dish and pour remaining sauce on top.
  5. Add cheese evenly over the enchiladas.
  6. Bake for 20–25 minutes until hot and bubbly.
  7. Serve warm (with sour cream, avocado, or cilantro).
